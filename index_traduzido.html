Frequently Asked Questions

Customized solutions and cutting-edge technology for your business.

1. How does the system development process work?

Our process is divided into clear steps to ensure delivery:
Diagnosis:We understand your needs and objectives.
Planning & UX:We create the technical scope and interface design.
Development:Coding using agile methodologies (Scrum/Kanban).
Tests (QA):Strict approval to ensure everything works error-free.
Launch & Support:We put the system online and followed the first steps.

2. Does the system use the Copyleft license? Will I have access to the source code?

Yes! SIRIUS values ​​the free software philosophy. Your project's source code will be delivered under the Copyleft model, ensuring you have full freedom to use, study, modify, and share the system, provided that modified versions retain these same freedoms.

3. What is the average deadline for software delivery?

MVPs (Minimum Viable Products):From 2 to 4 months.
Robust Systems/ERP:From 6 months to 1 year. We work with partial deliveries so that you can test and validate the system while it is still being built.

4. Do you offer post-launch maintenance and support?

For sure. We offer evolutionary support plans (for new features) and corrective support (to ensure system stability and security updates).

5. Can the system be integrated with other tools (APIs)?

Yes. We develop systems prepared to communicate with other platforms, such as payment gateways, CRMs, marketing automation tools, government systems or software that your company already uses.

6. How is data security guaranteed?

We follow the best information security practices and are in full compliance with the LGPD (General Data Protection Law). We use encryption, two-factor authentication and high reliability servers (such as AWS or Azure).
